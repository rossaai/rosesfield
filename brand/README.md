# Brand

This folder holds the Rosesfield logo and its variants.

- A fork may keep these files as part of its copy of this repository, under `../TRADEMARKS.md`.
- The icons made from the logo, such as the favicon and the installed app's icons, are covered the same way wherever they sit in the tree.

- `rosesfield-lockup-light.svg` and `rosesfield-lockup-dark.svg` are the logo: the field beside the name Rosesfield, in ink `#0C0A07` for light grounds and `#F7F4ED` for dark grounds.
- `rosesfield-icon-light.svg` and `rosesfield-icon-dark.svg` are the field alone, on a clear ground.
- `favicon.svg` is the field in the ink of the system theme. `favicon-16.png` and `favicon-32.png` are its fallbacks in light ink.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`, `rosesfield-icon-512-light.png` and `rosesfield-icon-512-dark.png` fill their square edge to edge, for the home screen and profile pictures.

The field is a hill under a sky, drawn with circle arcs on a 16-unit grid in a 48-unit square. The hill is one arc whose top sits at a third of the height. Six paths run from that top to the ground line, one every 16 units, and each path is half as wide as a row on the ground, so five rows and two edges narrow toward the hill's top. In the logo the field stands 32 units tall beside the name, which is Montserrat 400 drawn as outlines at a cap height of 22.4, with -0.05 em of letter spacing.

`apps/platform/scripts/write-icons.ts` renders the installed app's icons from `rosesfield-icon-light.svg`.
