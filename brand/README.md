- `rosesfield-lockup-light.svg` is the logo for light grounds: the flower of ROSSA AI, LLC beside the name Rosesfield, in ink `#0C0A07`.
- `rosesfield-lockup-dark.svg` is the same logo for dark grounds, in ink `#F7F4ED`.

The name is Montserrat 400 drawn as outlines, at a cap height of 22.4 on a 64-unit-high canvas, with -0.03 em of letter spacing. The small icon for the favicon, the app and the profile pictures lands here when it is designed.
