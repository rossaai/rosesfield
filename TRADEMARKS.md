# The Rosesfield name and logo

Rosesfield's code, docs, design tokens and writing rules are MIT. Take them, change them and ship them.

The name "Rosesfield" and the Rosesfield logo are trademarks of ROSSA AI, LLC. This page says how you can use them. Its purpose is that a person who sees the name knows where it comes from.

## You can, without asking

- Say what Rosesfield is. Write about it, review it, teach it, make videos and tutorials about it.
- Say that your project is based on Rosesfield, uses Rosesfield or works with Rosesfield.
- Give a talk, a meetup or a workshop about Rosesfield, with the name in its title.
- Fork it and keep the name and the logo in your copy. Contributing, learning, personal use and use inside your company need no change at all.
- Share your fork, changed or not, with the name and the logo, when its README says in one line that it is a fork of Rosesfield and not made by ROSSA AI, LLC.
- Use the logo, unmodified, to link to this project or to show that your work runs on it.

## Ask first

- Selling something, or hosting a service for other people, under the name or the logo. That includes a paid product, a hosted service, an app store listing and a domain name.
- Merchandise with the name or the logo.
- Anything that says or suggests that ROSSA AI, LLC made, approves or supports your work when it does not.

To ask, open an issue at https://github.com/rossaai/rosesfield.

## Forks

A fork is welcome, and you do not have to rename it. The one thing that matters is that nobody mistakes your fork for ours. When you share it, one line in its README is enough:

> This is a fork of Rosesfield. It is not made by ROSSA AI, LLC.
